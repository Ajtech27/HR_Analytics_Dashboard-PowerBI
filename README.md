# HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is a comprehensive **HR Analytics dashboard built in Power BI** to analyze workforce composition, employee attrition, performance, satisfaction, and career progression.  
The dashboard is designed to support **HR decision-making** around retention, engagement, workforce planning, and employee development.

The solution is presented across **three interactive dashboard pages**, each focusing on a key HR business area.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Understand overall workforce structure and demographics  
- Identify attrition and retrenchment patterns  
- Analyze employee performance, satisfaction, and promotion trends  
- Provide data-driven insights to improve HR policies and employee engagement  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 📄 Page 1: HR Overview
**Purpose:** Provide a high-level snapshot of the organization’s workforce.

**Key Metrics & Visuals**
- Total Employees
- Employee distribution by Job Level
- Gender and Marital Status distribution
- Years of Service overview

**Key Business Question**
> What does the current workforce look like?

---

### 📄 Page 2: Attrition & Retention Analysis
**Purpose:** Identify employee exit patterns and potential retention risks.

**Key Metrics & Visuals**
- Attrition status
- Retrenchment Status analysis

**Key Business Question**
> Who is leaving the organization and who is due for promotion?

---

### 📄 Page 3: Performance, Satisfaction & Growth
**Purpose:** Analyze employee engagement, performance, and career progression.

**Key Metrics & Visuals**
- Performance Rating distribution
- Job Satisfaction levels
- Career progression by Job role

**Key Business Question**
> How engaged are employees and how effectively are performance and growth managed?

---

## 🗂 Data Description
The project uses **two related tables**:

### 1️⃣ Employee Lookup Table
- Employee Name  
- Employee Number  

Used as a **dimension table** for identification and filtering.

### 2️⃣ HR Employee Data Table
Key fields include:
- Demographics: Age, Gender, Marital Status, Education, Education Field  
- Job Information: Department, Job Role, Job Level, Monthly Income  
- Experience: Total Working Years, Years at Company, Years in Current Role  
- Performance & Satisfaction: Performance Rating, Job Satisfaction, Work-Life Balance  
- Attrition & Status: Attrition, Retrenchment Status, Promotion Status, OverTime  

---

 ## 🧠 Key Insights
- Attrition varies across departments and job roles rather than being evenly distributed.
- Employees working overtime show a higher likelihood of attrition.
- Job satisfaction and work-life balance levels differ significantly across roles.
- Employees with longer tenure demonstrate higher retention.
- Career progression gaps exist for some employee groups based on promotion status and tenure.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Review overtime and workload distribution in high-attrition departments.
- Implement targeted engagement strategies for low-satisfaction roles.
- Strengthen promotion and career development pathways.
- Use satisfaction and tenure metrics to proactively manage retention risks.
- Align training initiatives with performance improvement goals.
